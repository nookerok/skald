SECTION 01

Player Journey — the information pipeline

What a player action becomes. The player's free-text intent is never an Event; only the world's response is. The player observes the response as presentation, belief and map — never the machinery.

The pipeline. Command is never an Event (AGENTS #7); Narrative/LLM is never authoritative (#4); the normal renderer reads only the Belief DTO and documented read DTOs.

What reaches the player

Game shell snapshot, turn presentation, belief model DTO, observer map, journal, observer threads, presence state — every one observer-scoped and derived from committed Events.

What never reaches the player

The raw Event Log, rule internals, spatial/weather/heat/settlement projections, pending checks, exact geometry of glimpsed landmarks. The no-truth-leak audit enforces it.

Why it feels like a world

Because the same pipeline runs while the player waits: TickPassed evolves weather, river, settlement — and the observer sees only the traces.

SECTION 03

What the player knows

Knowledge is a graduated field, not a binary. The belief model and the observer map share one vocabulary of knowing; information moves between categories as evidence arrives, ages, or is contradicted.

Traversed · known
Places walked through; facts confirmed by direct evidence with fresh, strong support.
confidence high · freshness high
Observed
Directly perceived: an examined object, a heard sound, a visible landmark. Evidence exists with a timestamp.
supportingEvidence · observedAt
Glimpsed
A bearing, a silhouette, a partial reading. Enough to point, not enough to place.
bearing · xMetres = null
Rumored
Reported but unverified; rumours can create contradictions between beliefs.
knowledge: rumored
Unknown
No evidence, no hypothesis, no map entry. The honest absence of knowledge.
not yet observed
Impossible to know
Constitutionally hidden: exact truth geometry, world-rule internals, the monolith's true meaning. Never rendered.
no-truth-leak enforced
Knowledge flows: hearsay → evidence → confirmation; age decays freshness; contradiction forces re-observation. The hidden tier never renders.

SECTION 06

Observer Map

The player's map shows knowledge, not geometry. Known area is drawn from observed evidence; everything else stays dark.

Fog of war over observer knowledge: traversed (green), observed (blue), glimpsed with a bearing (dashed), rumored/unknown stays dark. Exact coordinates of glimpsed landmarks never render.

What the DTO actually carries

For every entry: ref (opaque), name, knowledge (traversed/observed/glimpsed/rumored), confidence, freshness — and coordinates only where knowledge allows. Landmarks carry a bearing or null position. Routes are observed_path or a directional stub.

What is never sent

Terrain tiles, hidden simulation cells, landmark identities, exact position of merely-glimpsed subjects. The map is a knowledge surface, not a debug overlay.

source: ObserverMapDTO · region/observer-map.ts · ADR-0019

SECTION 09

Example session — one player, one turn at a time

Every screen below is a real, committed interaction in the current build.

1 · Enter the world

Known Worlds → presence entry → acknowledge → the shell unlocks. The world remembers a checkpoint.

2 · Observe

«examine old cart» → «Ты рассматриваешь old cart. A weathered wooden cart rests on one broken wheel.» Curiosity is recorded; belief forms.

3 · Act socially

«give help to guild» → «Твои связи с «Местная община» укрепились: Помощь.» The relation edge grows.

4 · Wait

Ticks pass. Weather changes, the river rises, the settlement drifts, the crossing becomes difficult. The player is absent; the world is not.

5 · Return to evidence

The journal lists what happened since. Belief freshness decays. Observer threads mark what is active or uncertain.

6 · The map moves

The observer map reveals what the player has earned: traversed ground, an observed city, a glimpsed monolith on a bearing.

7 · Form a hypothesis

«Почему река меняется?» — the player leaves with a question, not a quest.

SECTION 10

Architecture — one glance

SKALD in six layers. Truth is written once; knowledge is derived; presentation is honest; the player observes.

Simulation
World state, time, heat, geography — deterministic, Event Log is the only source of truth.
Rules
Pure functions (Event, ReadonlyWorld) → Event[]. No randomness, no network, no LLM.
Events
Append-only Domain Events with causation chains — the memory the world keeps.
Knowledge
Belief Model: observer-scoped, uncertain, aging, contradictable. The player's truth.
Presentation
Humanized, honest text and map. Never selects facts, never invents.
Player
Observes, hypothesizes, acts. The world answers with new events.
The loop is: Enter → Observe → Hypothesize → Interact → World responds → Collect evidence → Update understanding → Observe deeper.
SECTION 11

Pilot Region Gameplay Demonstration — why a player cares

The system works; this is why anyone would want to live in it. Attachment is systemic, not emotional: Observation → Knowledge → Prediction → Intervention → Consequence → Attachment. The demonstration is the actual pilot region, «Бассейн Речного Стража», day by day. LIVE beats run in the current build; FUTURE beats are honest design intent, not runtime.

The investment model: care is the product of lawfulness discovered through attention.
Day 1 · Arrival LIVE
Presence entry at «Переправа у Чёрного леса». The world knows you arrived. On the observer map: the waystation is traversed, two roads observed. To the north-east, a silhouette: «Парящий монолит» — glimpsed, a bearing, confidence 0.45, no coordinates.
sources: presence flow · ObserverMapDTO · region bootstrap (SpatialObservationRecorded)
Day 2 · First evidence LIVE
«examine …» builds an observation and a belief pattern with supporting evidence. The Знания rail shows confidence and freshness, not truth.
sources: perception · examinedCuriosity · BeliefModelDTO
Day 3 · The river answers LIVE
TickPassed advances the river. The ford crosses the difficult threshold: «Река поднялась: переправа стала трудной.» The player reads a consequence of a law they have not yet named.
sources: hydrology.river_level → hydrology.crossing_condition → presentation
Day 5 · The settlement breathes LIVE
«Речной Страж» drifts: risk decays, population shifts. A SettlementStateChanged reaches the journal. The city is a living pattern, not an NPC hub.
source: settlement.pattern
Day 8 · A hypothesis forms LIVE
The river falls again. Belief openHypotheses: «река следует циклу». No quest told you this — the system showed it.
source: belief openHypotheses over observed events
Day 12 · Prediction pays LIVE
You predict the ford will reopen at the cycle's trough — and it does. Knowledge became a tool: you can plan travel around the river. This is the core loop made tangible.
source: deterministic periodic river level (ADR-0017)
Day 30 · Memory fades and returns LIVE
Observer threads mark the river and weather as active processes. Belief freshness decays; a re-observation restores it. The world keeps a record of what you paid attention to.
sources: observer-threads · belief freshness
Day 90 · The first confirmation FUTURE
The full arc — you opened a mine upstream, the river changed, fields shrank, people left — is the shape this model takes when Migration and Economy connect. Today the law you confirmed is simpler: the river is periodic, and you can live with it.
future: P13 economy · P14 migration · P15 information · ADR-0023 Exposure
Attachment is the product of Prediction: a world whose laws you can discover and rely on is a world you can care about. This is exactly what the live beats above already deliver.

First Discovery Arc — not a quest, a law

SKALD's discovery is the shape of scientific method: observe, hypothesize, gather evidence, confirm. The arc below is real and runnable today — it is the river's periodicity. Its deeper form (the river was engineered) is Future canon.

The deeper arc — Future

When more processes connect, the same method points further: the ruins align with the river's course; the river behaves like something shaped, not formed. «The river was engineered» is a Future hypothesis (P13/P15, ADR-0023 Exposure) — the shape of the arc, not its current content.
